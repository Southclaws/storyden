Robots are Storyden's optional AI assistants. They can help your team find information, curate the Library and work with community content. You can chat with Denbot, create a specialist for a particular job, or use [Trails](/docs/introduction/trails) to run a task automatically.

The forum, Library and ordinary moderation tools work without Robots. On a fresh installation, start with [Set up Robots](/docs/introduction/robots/setup) to connect a model provider and choose who can use it.

## For the people looking after the community

Robots are generally intended for administrators, moderators and other trusted members with elevated privileges. They use the operator's model-provider credits and can act on community content, so making them available to everyone can introduce cost and abuse problems. Access is controlled through roles; start with your team and extend it deliberately.

Robot conversations are shared with members who have the **Use robots** [permission](/docs/introduction/members/permissions). Multiple members can participate in the same session at the same time: a group chat with one Robot. They are a shared working area, not private personal chats.

## Find, understand and work with your content

A Robot can find useful Library pages, read a discussion and help prepare a summary, or use the tools you assign to carry out a more specific task. Tool calls and linked sources stay with the conversation so you can inspect how it reached an answer.

**Denbot** is the built-in starting point for conversations. It can discover tools and delegate focused tasks to specialist Robots. You can reach it from anywhere in Storyden with **Ctrl+K** (**Cmd+K** on macOS), then choose **Robot Chat** from the [command palette](/docs/introduction/command-palette).

A **custom Robot** keeps a playbook, model and tool selection together for a repeatable job. **Toolsets** bundle capabilities and instructions that several Robots can reuse.

Robots can also use tools supplied by plugins, and plugins can call Robots. See [Connections and plugins](/docs/introduction/robots/connections) for how these directions fit together.
