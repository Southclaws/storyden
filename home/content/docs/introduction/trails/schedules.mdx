After [creating your first Trail](/docs/introduction/trails/getting-started), use **Edit → Trigger → Schedule** to change when it runs.

## Cadence

| Cadence                     | Example                                                                   |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **One time**                | Review a set of submissions on a particular date.                         |
| **Every few hours or days** | Check for material needing attention every six hours.                     |
| **Selected weekdays**       | Prepare a reading list every Monday, or every other Tuesday and Thursday. |
| **Monthly**                 | Review a directory on a selected day or the last day of each month.       |
| **Yearly**                  | Prepare material for an annual community event.                           |

Calendar dates that do not exist are skipped. A monthly schedule for the 31st does not run in February; choose the last day of the month if that is what you mean.

## Timezones and previews

Choose the timezone whose local clock the schedule should follow. A weekly 09:00 schedule remains at 09:00 there when daylight saving time changes. Nonexistent local times during a clock change are skipped; repeated local times run once.

Use **Preview next five** after changing the cadence, timezone, starting date or time. The preview shows the upcoming occurrences before you save.

## Pauses and downtime

Pausing stops new automatic runs. **Run now** starts a separate manual run without shifting the schedule. When the scheduler returns after downtime, missed scheduled work is recorded as skipped instead of running a backlog. See [Runs and results](/docs/introduction/trails/runs) for how this appears in history.
