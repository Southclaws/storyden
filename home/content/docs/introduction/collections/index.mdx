---
title: Collections
description: Curate and collect content across the community.
---

Collections allow members to organise their favourite content from a Storyden site into folders. Threads and Pages may be added to collections and collections themselves have titles, descriptions and cover images. Just like other content in Storyden, collections can be shared and interlinked via the Datagraph.

Open **Collections** to create a collection with a name and description. Use the bookmark action on a thread or the collection action on a Library page to add content to it.

![A Design inspiration collection containing a Library page and community threads](/docs/introduction/collections/collection.png)

Collections keep related discussions and reference material together, so members can return to a topic without searching the whole community.
