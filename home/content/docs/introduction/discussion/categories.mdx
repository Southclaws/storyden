---
title: Categories
description: Where discussions are organised.
---

Threads can belong to a category or remain uncategorised.

Categories help keep discussions organised. They’re broad groupings like “Writing”, “Movies”, or “Off-topic”.

Members cannot create categories unless they hold the "Manage Categories" permission.

![General and Tech categories with cover images, descriptions and thread counts](/docs/introduction/discussion/categories.png)

Members with **Manage categories** can use a category's **More options → Edit** menu to change its cover image, name, URL slug, description and colour.

![Editing the General category’s cover, description and colour](/docs/introduction/discussion/category-edit.png)
