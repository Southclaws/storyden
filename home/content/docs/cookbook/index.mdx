The Cookbook contains practical guides and patterns for building and running communities with Storyden. These recipes show you how to use Storyden's features together to do neat things.

## What's in the Cookbook?

Unlike the introduction and reference, cookbook recipes show you how to compose Storyden features.

Each recipe is designed to be:

- **Complete**: shows the full thing from start to finish
- **Practical**: based on real-world stuff
- **Actionable**: includes specific steps you can follow

## Robots and Trails

These recipes combine a focused Robot with a schedule or event trigger. They use Storyden's built-in tools and keep their results available for your team to review. Start with [Robot setup](/docs/introduction/robots/setup) if this is a fresh installation.
